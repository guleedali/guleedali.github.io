Laila Elbaroudy presented *Reconciling Radiocarbon and Cosmogenic Chronologies of the Laurentide Ice Sheet Retreat Across Long Island, New York* ([poster 55-4](https://gsameetings.secure-platform.com/connects26/gallery/rounds/82026/details/26441)) in the session on the extent and timing of Late Quaternary glaciations. The work reports the first radiocarbon ages from a kettle basin in eastern Long Island and is supported by a GSA Northeastern Section Undergraduate Research Grant.
